Diversification across asset classes helps reduce idiosyncratic risk.
Investors with higher risk aversion often prefer larger allocations to high-quality bonds and cash equivalents.
Long investment horizons generally allow more equity exposure because short-term volatility can be absorbed over time.
Rebalancing periodically can maintain target risk levels and avoid drift.
Sharpe ratio compares excess return to volatility and can be used to compare risk-adjusted performance.
